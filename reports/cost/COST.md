## Definitions

- `gpu_h_measured`: wall time x the GPUs a step's processes ran on, from a step record (NVML's process list). The number to compare methods on.
- `gpu_h_reconstructed`: wall time x the job's allocated GPUs, for a step rebuilt from a log. CSCS bills whole nodes, so it is what the step held, and an upper bound on what it used. Quote it as "allocated GPU-h (reconstructed from Slurm and job logs)", never in one column with measured use.
- `idle`: allocated minus used, for jobs with step records only. A reconstructed job's idle time is inside its steps' `gpu_h_reconstructed`.
- `gpu_h_not_ok`: steps that failed, hit the time limit, were cancelled or killed. A training or generation job resumes from its checkpoints and cached chunks, so this bounds the waste from above.
- `gpu_energy_kwh`: NVML energy of the GPUs a recorded step used; blank where nothing was measured. `node_energy_kwh` (jobs.csv) is Slurm's whole-node figure.
- `recipe_gpu_h` (by arm): what producing the arm once takes -- its data generation and training, shared steps in full; failed, cancelled and killed attempts and checks of the data left out, segments cut by the time limit kept (`recipe` in steps.csv says which steps count). Blank: the arm's data and training are not in these jobs.

## Stating a measured claim

- **Speed: the same work done differently.** Two steps doing identical work (the same prompts and decoding, the same training data and schedule) that differ only in a setting, the software or the hardware -- prefix caching on or off, batch order, one engine or another. Faster by `1 - t_new / t_ref`, on `generate_s` for steady-state throughput (it leaves out `load_s`) or on `gpu_h_measured` end to end; say which. Run each condition at least twice and give the median and range.
- **Methods: cost to reach an effect.** Methods do different work per step (a GRPO step generates rollouts, DPO computes a reference pass), so equal steps are not equal work. Compare them from a common starting checkpoint, on the same evaluation, as the GPU-hours each needed to reach the checkpoint that was audited (`checkpoints.csv`, or `recipe_gpu_h` for a whole run), next to the effect.
- **Parameters**: fewer by `1 - p_new / p_ref`, from `params_trainable` or `params_modified`, against the same base (`params_base_text`).
- Quote both rows' job IDs and the basis (measured or reconstructed).
